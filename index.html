We're an editing-only service. You send us raw footage or UGC — your own, or from creators and agencies you already work with — and we turn it into finished ad creative. We don't offer filming, shoots, or on-location production.

Standard turnaround is 24 hours for ad edits and short-form. On Sloop and above, standard requests can be delivered same-day within a single edit cycle. VSLs and long-form typically take 2–3 business days. Exact speed depends on footage volume and how much motion graphics work is requested.

Yes. Once matched, your editor is dedicated to your account — they learn your brand, your pacing, and your ad style over time instead of starting from zero on every request. A second trained editor is kept on standby for coverage, but you always work with the same primary editor.

Every editor goes through a screening and test-edit process before working with clients, and is trained specifically on direct-response and performance-editing conventions — hooks, pacing, captions, platform specs — not just general video editing. They're also professional and easy to work with: clear communicators, responsive to feedback, and respectful of your time and brand. If a working relationship ever isn't clicking, we handle the re-match, not you.
